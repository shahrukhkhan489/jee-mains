### Mathematics Strategy & Key Takeaways
1. **The Mega Duo (Vectors & 3D Geometry):** Together, `Vector Algebra` and `3D Geometry` account for **4 to 5 questions per shift** (over 16-20 marks). These follow standard algorithms and should never be skipped.
2. **Coordinate Geometry Hegemony:** With **71-75 questions per year** (averaging 3-4 questions in every single shift), Coordinate Geometry (`Straight Lines`, `Circles`, `Parabola`, `Ellipse`, `Hyperbola`) is the largest single scoring unit in Mathematics.
3. **Calculus Synergy:** `Definite Integration`, `Differential Equations`, and `Area Under the Curve` provide consistent, predictable scoring opportunities once standard integral properties and linear differential equation methods are mastered.
4. **Independent High-Yield Modules:** `Matrices & Determinants`, `Sequence & Series`, and `Statistics` are self-contained topics with virtually guaranteed questions in every session.
