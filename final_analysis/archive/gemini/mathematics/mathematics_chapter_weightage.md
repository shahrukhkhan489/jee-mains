## High-Yield Scoring Strategy for Mathematics
1. **The 4 Pillars of 50+ Marks:**
   - **Coordinate Geometry** (Straight Lines, Circles, Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola) = 3 to 4 questions guaranteed.
   - **Vectors & 3D Geometry** = 3 to 4 questions guaranteed.
   - **Matrices & Determinants** = 1 to 2 questions guaranteed.
   - **Sequence & Series** = 1 to 2 questions guaranteed.
   Mastering just these 4 areas yields **9 to 12 questions (36 to 48 marks)**.
2. **Calculus Precision:** Prioritize `Definite Integration` and `Differential Equations`. In indefinite integration, question frequency is low (only 0-1 question), while definite integrals with King's Property or Leibniz Rule appear in every shift.
3. **Quick Wins:** `Statistics` (variance/standard deviation calculations) and `Area Under Curve` are short chapters that consistently yield 1 direct question each.
