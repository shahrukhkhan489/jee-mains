## High-Yield Scoring Strategy for Chemistry
1. **The Inorganic Fast Marks (30+ Marks in 15 Minutes):**
   - `Coordination Compounds` (2-3 questions) + `Chemical Bonding` (1-2 questions) + `d- and f-Block Elements` (1-2 questions) + `Periodic Table` (1 question).
   These are 100% NCERT line-by-line questions with zero numerical calculation required.
2. **Organic Engine:** Master `General Organic Chemistry (GOC)` first. All reaction mechanisms (Aldol, Cannizzaro, nucleophilic substitution, diazonium coupling) depend on carbocation stability and resonance.
3. **High-ROI Memorization Units:** `Biomolecules` (carbohydrates, amino acids, vitamins) is a guaranteed 4 marks per shift.
