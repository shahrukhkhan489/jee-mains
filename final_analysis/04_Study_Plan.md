# 04 - Study Plan

The phase structure is adapted from Gemini's study plan; the chapter priorities come from the lecture 2024-2026 data. Target marks are editorial, not data.

## Phase 1 - Quick-win foundation

- **Physics:** Units & Measurements + Dual Nature + Atoms & Nuclei + Semiconductors (~20 marks).
- **Chemistry:** Biomolecules + Periodic Table + Coordination Compounds + Chemical Bonding.
- **Mathematics:** Matrices & Determinants + Vector Algebra + Statistics + Sequence & Series.

## Phase 2 - Core heavyweights

- **Mathematics:** Coordinate Geometry (including Conic Sections) + Three-Dimensional Geometry.
- **Physics:** Heat & Thermodynamics + Current Electricity + Electrostatics + Properties of Matter.
- **Chemistry:** GOC (including Isomerism) + Aldehydes/Ketones/Carboxylic Acids + d- and f-Block + Electrochemistry.

## Phase 3 - Targeted finishers

- **Mathematics:** Definite Integration + Differential Equations + Area Under the Curve + Binomial Theorem.
- **Physics:** Geometrical Optics + Wave Optics + Kinematics + Gravitation.
- **Chemistry:** Thermodynamics + Chemical Kinetics + Solutions + Amines.
