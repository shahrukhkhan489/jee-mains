## What stands out

- **Mathematics:** Coordinate Geometry is the single largest block (851 questions, 4.92 per paper). Sets/Relations/Functions, Definite Integration, Trigonometry and AOD follow.
- **Physics:** Current Electricity (514), Kinematics (459) and Heat & Thermodynamics (453) lead; Magnetic Effects, Laws of Motion, Work-Power-Energy and Electrostatics form the next tier.
- **Chemistry:** Chemical Bonding, Some Basic Concepts (Mole), Thermodynamics, Coordination Compounds and Solutions lead the classified set. **Treat organic counts as lower bounds** - see the coverage warning below.

> **Chemistry coverage warning.** Chemistry is 76.2% classified. Most of its 1,091 unclassified questions are organic reaction-scheme questions whose reagents/products are images in the PDF text layer, so GOC, Hydrocarbons, Haloalkanes, Alcohols and Amines are undercounted. Do not rank those chapters low from this table alone; use the lecture weightage in `docs/02_Chapterwise_Weightage_Analysis.md` as a cross-check.

> **How to read the tables.** `Questions` is the count of classified questions. `Avg/paper` = questions divided by 173 papers, which is the fair comparison because old papers have different patterns (2002: 225 questions; 2006: 150; 2007: 120; 2008: 105; most others 75 or 90). `% subject` uses only classified questions in that subject.

## Method and limits

- Text was extracted in reading order from each PDF (`pdftotext -bbox-layout`), cut at `ANSWER KEYS`, and split into question blocks by `Q1..Qn`.
- Subject sections were detected per paper by evidence scoring (2025 papers are Mathematics > Physics > Chemistry; all older papers are Physics > Chemistry > Mathematics). Each question was then classified only inside its section subject.
- Chapters are assigned by a weighted keyword/notation classifier built from the 65 chapter names in the lecture weightage. Every question gets one chapter; no question is counted twice.
- **Unclassified** means the question text did not contain enough subject-specific words after extraction (most are organic reaction-scheme questions whose reagents/products are images, or formula-only numericals). It is reported separately and excluded from chapter percentages.
- **Outside the 65-chapter list** covers topics the lecture list omits but old papers contain: polymers, surface chemistry, chemistry in everyday life, environmental chemistry, hydrogen/s-block, solid state. They are not forced into a wrong chapter.
- Definite and indefinite integrals both render as the same `∫` in the PDF text layer. Questions with only the symbol are counted under Definite Integration; the Indefinite row captures only questions with explicit wording.
- The classifier is deterministic and auditable: every assignment is in `question_chapter_map.csv` with its keyword score and margin. A 15-question random spot-check produced 14 clear matches and 1 defensible alternative (area question placed in Coordinate Geometry rather than AUC).
- Paper order in `paper_summary.csv` is newest to oldest. Raw totals are dominated by old large papers; use Avg/paper or % subject for study priorities.
