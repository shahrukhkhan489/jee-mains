# High-Yield JEE Main Syllabus Mastery Plan

> **Actionable Roadmap:** Designed based on empirical shift data to take an aspirant from 0 to 99th percentile by prioritizing chapters that maximize score return per hour of study.

---

## Phase 1: The 'Quick-Win' Foundation (Target: 80–100 Marks)
Focus on chapters with **short syllabus + formula-based questions + 100% guarantee of appearing**:

1. **Physics:** `Units & Measurements` + `Dual Nature of Radiation` + `Atoms and Nuclei` + `Semiconductors` (~20 Marks, 4–5 days).
2. **Chemistry:** `Biomolecules` + `Periodic Table & Periodicity` + `Coordination Compounds` + `Chemical Bonding` (~24–28 Marks, 5–6 days).
3. **Mathematics:** `Matrices & Determinants` + `Vector Algebra` + `Statistics` + `Sequence & Series` (~24–28 Marks, 5–6 days).

---

## Phase 2: The 'Core Heavyweights' (Target: 160–190 Marks)
Conquer the single highest-frequency units that decide rank:

1. **Mathematics:** `Coordinate Geometry` (Straight Lines, Circles, Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola) + `Three-Dimensional Geometry (3D)` (~28–32 Marks).
2. **Physics:** `Heat and Thermodynamics` + `Current Electricity` + `Electrostatics` + `Properties of Matter` (~28–32 Marks).
3. **Chemistry:** `Basics of Organic Chemistry (GOC)` + `Aldehydes, Ketones & Carboxylic Acids` + `d- and f-Block Elements` + `Electrochemistry` (~28–32 Marks).

---

## Phase 3: Targeted Scoring Finishers (Target: 220+ Marks)
1. **Mathematics:** `Definite Integration` (King's Property) + `Differential Equations` + `Area Under Curve` + `Binomial Theorem`.
2. **Physics:** `Geometrical Optics` + `Wave Optics` + `Kinematics` + `Gravitation`.
3. **Chemistry:** `Thermodynamics` + `Chemical Kinetics` + `Solutions` + `Amines`.
