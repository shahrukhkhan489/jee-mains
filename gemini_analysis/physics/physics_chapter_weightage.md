## High-Yield Scoring Strategy for Physics
1. **Modern Physics Super-Block (20 Marks Guaranteed):**
   - `Units & Measurements` + `Dual Nature` + `Atoms and Nuclei` + `Semiconductors`.
   These 4 chapters consistently yield **5 questions per shift** (~20 marks). Questions are direct formula substitutions (de Broglie wavelength, Bohr radius/energy formulas, logic gates truth tables, dimensional analysis).
2. **Core Physical Heavyweights:** `Heat and Thermodynamics` (2-3 questions) and `Current Electricity` (1-2 questions, surging trend) form the bedrock of scoring.
3. **Optics Advantage:** Ray Optics + Wave Optics together guarantee **2 to 3 questions per shift**.
